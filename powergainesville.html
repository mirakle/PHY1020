Your mission

Gainesville wants a small neighborhood to run on clean electricity. Your group will follow the energy from sunlight to the power grid: how sunlight travels, why extra greenhouse gases warm the planet, and whether solar panels or wind turbines are the better choice here.

  • Work as a group of up to 4 students. Discuss every question before you answer.
  • Type numbers only: no units, commas, dollar signs or percent signs.
  • Round as each question asks. Answers within about 1% of the exact value are accepted.
  • You have 3 attempts on number questions and 2 attempts on multiple-choice questions. A worked solution appears after a correct answer or your last attempt.
  • As in lecture, one month = 30 days.
  • Your numbers are different from other groups’ numbers. Progress is saved in this browser.
  • When every question is finished, enter your names to get your completion certificate.

Completion certificate

Enter the name of each student in your group (1 to 4 names). Only the first box is required.

To submit: take a screenshot of the certificate and upload it to Canvas, or copy the certificate text and paste it into your submission.

Replace the coal plant

A city of 200,000 people is closing its old coal plant, which supplies 40% of its electricity. Build a new mix that adds up to 100%, then see how each group in town reacts.

    Questions for your group

    • Assign each person a role card. Can your group find a mix that every role supports? What did you give up?
    • Run all three night tests. Which one is hardest for your mix, and why? What could fill the gap besides fossil fuels?
    • Nuclear has very low emissions and deaths per unit of energy, but it is expensive to build. How should a city weigh those?

    About the numbers

    Figures are rounded for teaching. Emissions are lifecycle medians from the IPCC; deaths per unit of electricity are from Our World in Data; new-plant costs are approximate midpoints from Lazard (2024) and IRENA; household use is from the US EIA. Check costs before class, since they change often.
